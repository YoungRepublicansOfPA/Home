Find a church for Sunday worship

If it has been a while since you went to church, consider making room this Sunday to pray, hear Scripture and worship Jesus Christ with a congregation. You can come on your own or invite a friend. The visitor pages below give service times and directions.

Philadelphia County

These two congregations belong to Christian traditions that reserve particular ordained offices for men. Each describes those offices in its own terms.

Cathedral Basilica of Saints Peter and Paul

This Roman Catholic parish is the mother church of the Archdiocese of Philadelphia. Its Sunday schedule includes Mass in English and Spanish. Catholic canon 1024 reserves sacred ordination for baptized men.

Tenth Presbyterian Church

This congregation meets at 17th and Spruce Streets. Its morning and evening services include preaching and traditional music. Tenth belongs to the Presbyterian Church in America, whose Book of Church Order, section 7-2, reserves the ordained offices of elder and deacon for men.

What “ministry” meant in different traditions

Ordination, preaching and lay service have distinct histories. Seventeenth-century Quaker Margaret Fell, for example, published Womens Speaking Justified in 1666, defending women speaking on scriptural grounds. Protestant traditions differed on this question centuries before modern debates. Within Catholicism, women have also led religious communities and served as educators and missionaries. Philadelphia’s cathedral houses the shrine of St. Katharine Drexel, a local example.
