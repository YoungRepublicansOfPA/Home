Pennsylvania population through time

Pennsylvania gained 300,321 residents between 2010 and 2020. Population grew in 23 counties and fell in 44. Choose a county to see its longer history. The change column compares each count with the preceding census shown.

How Pennsylvania’s age profile changed

Statewide, the number of residents aged 65 and older rose by 523,747 between 2010 and 2020. The number under 18 fell by 143,003 over the same decade.
