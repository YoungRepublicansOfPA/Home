Our method

Transparent by design

We are building a useful database, one verified record at a time. A link to a source is not the same as a checked claim about an officeholder, a budget amount, or a bill’s status.

Current release: all 67 county source directories are live; selected budget figures, public meetings, and state bills have been checked. Additional officeholders and county measures are being added.

Every future record should include

  • Jurisdiction and office or measure
  • Primary source URL and document date
  • Date checked and status of verification
  • Budget year and units for financial amounts
  • Bill number, chamber, session, and latest action
  • A visible correction history when a record changes

Editorial priorities

We expect to pay particular attention to youth opportunity, family stability, public spending, and civic liberty. Those are editorial choices. Source documents and factual fields should remain visible regardless of viewpoint.
