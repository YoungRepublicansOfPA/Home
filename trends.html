Civic history

A founding place

Philadelphia served as the interim federal capital from 1790 to 1800. That history is a starting point for asking what kind of Pennsylvania we want to build now.

1790–1800

Federal capital

Congress met in Philadelphia while the permanent capital was being built.

U.S. House history

1904–1950

Chestnut lost from the canopy

Blight was first identified in 1904. By 1950, American chestnut had largely disappeared as a forest canopy tree across its range. Living sprouts remain, and restoration research continues.

USDA Forest Service · PA DCNR

2026

Restoration research

USDA APHIS deregulated the Darling 54 blight-tolerant chestnut under its plant-pest rules. That decision does not establish broad forest restoration.

USDA APHIS, Aug. 27, 2026

Public land and nature

Protect places we share

We are tracking measurable park conditions and conservation policy. Available records do not establish a current statewide trend in graffiti or identify who causes damage.

State park use

38.6 million

Visitor days in 2025, versus 38.5 million in 2024 in the same state financial report. Visitor days are not unique visitors. The report marks both years with a footnote; figures from other DCNR pages may use different revisions.

PA Annual Comprehensive Financial Report, Table 17, p. 288

Graffiti and vandalism

DCNR documented these as management challenges and reported more than 1,000 volunteer hours cleaning graffiti in state parks and forests during 2016–2018. That is a historical cleanup measure, not a count of incidents or proof that damage is rising now.

PA DCNR Outdoor Recreation Plan, pp. 56–57

Wild leeks, or ramps

DCNR says ramps can take five to seven years to mature and produce seed. Gathering rules depend on the land. State park rules generally bar removing plants without written permission; the personal-use exception names fruits, nuts, berries, and fungi, not ramps. State forest rules have a separate personal or family-use allowance for edible wild plants, subject to protected-species limits. Check the land manager and species status before harvesting.

DCNR Forest Focus, Spring 2023 · State park rule · State forest rules

Next research agenda

What a household can build

Our concern is whether work supports a healthy life, enough family time, and the freedom to make and grow things locally. These are questions for evidence and policy debate, not conclusions drawn from one historical comparison.

The cost of going to work

Pennsylvania workers reported a 26.3-minute mean commute to work in the 2020–2024 ACS. A 2025 state survey put median child-care provider cost at $370 per child per week, compared with $290 in its 2019 survey. Provider costs are not necessarily what parents paid; survey samples differ. Next, compare these costs alongside wages, taxes, and housing by county.

Census QuickFacts · PA DHS child-care cost survey

Movement and health at work

Compare physically demanding, sedentary, and flexible work using injury, chronic-health, and time-use measures. More movement can help, while some occupational exertion or prolonged standing carries risk. Older jobs also carried serious physical risks.

CDC NIOSH on occupational physical activity

Food and household life

Track grocery prices, access to fresh food, family meal time, and opportunities to garden. Pennsylvania publishes retail food inspection information; individual inspections are snapshots and do not establish a broad food trend. Health claims need a named substance, exposure measure, and evidence.

PA Department of Agriculture food safety

Local enterprise and family time

Census counted 1,001,577 Pennsylvania nonemployer establishments in 2024. Study business formation, home-based work, access to capital, and child care. Explore how AI tools might help families and community businesses while measuring actual outcomes.

Census QuickFacts, nonemployer establishments

Education and parental trust

Pennsylvania regulation gives parents access to curriculum, instructional materials, and assessments, with a review process. We will link public board agendas, school spending, academic outcomes, and actual materials when discussing what a school teaches.

How to read this page

Evidence before explanation

Our interest in family formation, affordability, shared language, public land, and local heritage guides what we research. A measured change does not, by itself, establish its cause. We will not attribute park damage or plant harvesting to a demographic group without evidence. County-level comparisons and policy links are still being built.
