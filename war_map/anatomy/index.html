    How these numbers work

    Documented seats have a status we can show from a public record: a committee roster or official committee election results. Missing seats are ones whose status couldn't be determined, such as seats won only by unnamed write-ins. Empty and filled shares are taken out of documented seats only.

    A state shows no percentages until its seats are counted from official sources. A War Map can be live before its committee seats are documented. Pennsylvania figures come from county rosters and official results, county by county, and update when that folder does. Precinct results come from The New York Times 2024 dataset; see each state for coverage gaps.
