# Pennsylvania district geography

These statewide layers display the boundaries for the 2025–2026 Pennsylvania General Assembly and the 119th U.S. Congress (2025–2027). The Pennsylvania Legislative Reapportionment Commission approved the final state House and Senate plans on February 4, 2022. The Pennsylvania Supreme Court adopted the congressional map on February 23, 2022. The state's [official redistricting maps and legal descriptions](https://www.redistricting.state.pa.us/maps/) identify the underlying plans.

Retrieved November 24, 2026. Each feature stores the integer PennDOT LEG_DISTRICT_NO as properties.district. Coordinates use WGS84 longitude and latitude. PennDOT served the geometry with a maximum allowable offset of 0.0001 degrees and five decimal places of precision for map display. For an address close to a boundary, use the [General Assembly district finder](https://www.palegis.us/find-my-legislator).
