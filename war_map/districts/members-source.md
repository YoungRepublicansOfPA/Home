The state district lists use the atlas’s [November 24 state roster](../../pa-legislators.json). Members serving more than one county are counted once by district. The federal list uses the Pennsylvania table in the U.S. House directory. Each directory supplied an officeholder for every numbered district at the time checked.

Sitting officeholders can differ from November 2026 election winners. For example, Pennsylvania House District 98 lists Tom Jones for the current term. His official House profile and the House Archives identify his service in that district in 2026.
