Follow public funding to a local program

Start with the municipality and school district serving your address. Housing authorities, county offices and school districts can serve different boundaries. Find your municipality by address · Find your school district

Rental assistance

HUD funds Housing Choice Vouchers. A local public housing agency administers the voucher and pays the housing assistance portion of rent to the owner. A participating household generally contributes toward the rent. Search Pennsylvania in the agency directory and confirm the service area. Voucher program · Local housing authorities

Heating and energy

Federal LIHEAP grants support Pennsylvania’s heating assistance program. Pennsylvania DHS administers assistance through county offices. A cash grant generally goes directly to the fuel supplier, and crisis grants address eligible heating emergencies. PA LIHEAP · County assistance offices

Child care

Child Care Works uses state and federal funds to subsidize care for eligible families. Early Learning Resource Centers administer the program locally. A family may pay a copayment to its provider. Program funding and regional contacts

School funding and meals

School district annual financial reports show local, state and federal revenue and district expenditures by school year. USDA also publishes federal child nutrition tables. For a spending total, reconcile meal reimbursements with district accounts before combining the two. District budgets cover a wider population than income-tested assistance programs. District financial reports · Federal school meal tables

School transportation

Districts report pupil transportation activity and costs to the Pennsylvania Department of Education. The state calculates a transportation subsidy. Compare the district’s transportation expense with the state payment for the relevant reporting and payment periods. Transportation reporting · Subsidy payment schedule

Home care and family caregivers

Pennsylvania’s Caregiver Support Program connects eligible households with respite and reimbursement for approved care expenses through their Area Agency on Aging. Medicaid’s Community HealthChoices also covers home and community services for eligible participants through state and federal Medicaid funding. When using statewide Medicaid totals, check whether home care payments already appear within them. Caregiver support and local aging agencies · Community HealthChoices
